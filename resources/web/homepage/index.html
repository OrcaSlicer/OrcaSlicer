Recommended order
Per printer
Cornering
When: corners look rounded or wavy
Measures speed lost at sharp direction changes.
Input shaping frequency
When: once per printer
Finds the machine's resonant frequency so input shaping can cancel ringing.
Input shaping damping
When: after the frequency is set
Tunes how hard that ringing gets cancelled.
VFA
When: fast prints look rough on the surface
Measures vertical fine artifacts left by high-speed moves.
Per filament
Temperature
When: corners blob or detail is stringy
Finds the best nozzle temperature for this filament.
Max volumetric speed
When: before raising speed limits
Finds the fastest the hotend can melt without starving.
Pressure advance
When: after changing extruder, filament or temps
Tunes how far the nozzle moves ahead of its own pressure.
Flow ratio
When: for thin or inconsistent extrusion
Measures how much material really lands on the build plate.
Retraction
When: stringing issues
Finds the shortest retraction that still stops ooze during travel.
