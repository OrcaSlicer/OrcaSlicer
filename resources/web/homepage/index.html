Recommended Test Order
Per filament
Temperature
When: Corners blob or detail is stringy
Order: First test for a new filament.
Max volumetric speed
When: Parts are under-extruded or the nozzle clogs
Order: After temperature. Before changing flow speed.
Pressure advance
When: After changing extruder, filament or temps
Order: After temperature, before flow ratio.
Flow ratio
When: For thin or inconsistent extrusion
Order: After pressure advance.
Retraction Optional
When: Stringing issues
Order: After pressure advance and flow ratio.
Per printer
Cornering Optional Advanced
When: Corners look rounded or wavy. You want to push print speed.
Order: First of the advanced tests, after the filament tests.
Input shaping frequency Optional Advanced
When: Once per printer Use automatic input shaping instead if your printer supports it.
Order: After the filament tests.
Input shaping damping Optional Advanced
When: Ringing is still visible in prints
Order: After the frequency is set.
VFA Optional Advanced
When: Fast prints look rough on the surface
Order: After input shaping.
